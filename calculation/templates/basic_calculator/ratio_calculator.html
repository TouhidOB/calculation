What Is a Ratio?

A ratio compares two quantities, showing how much of one there is relative to another, written A:B or A to B. A ratio of 15:25 means 15 units of the first for every 25 of the second. Ratios describe mixtures, scales, and proportions. Like fractions, they're clearest in lowest terms — the goal of the simplification calculation.

How Do You Simplify a Ratio?

Divide both parts by their greatest common factor. For 15:25, the GCF is 5, so 15÷5 : 25÷5 gives 3:5. This mirrors fraction reduction, since a ratio A:B is essentially the fraction A/B. The calculator finds the GCF using the Euclidean algorithm and reduces both parts in one calculation.

What Is the Decimal Value of a Ratio?

Dividing A by B converts a ratio to a single decimal, useful for comparison. The ratio 3:5 equals 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6. This decimal form lets you quickly compare ratios that look different — 3:5 and 6:10 both reduce to 0.6, revealing they're equal. The calculator shows this decimal alongside the simplified ratio.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you simplify 15:25?

Divide both by their GCF of 5, giving 3:5. The calculator returns the simplified ratio and its decimal value of 0.6 instantly.

Is a ratio the same as a fraction?

They're closely related. The ratio A:B corresponds to the fraction A/B and simplifies the same way, but ratios emphasize comparison rather than a part of a whole.

Where are ratios used?

Recipes, screen aspect ratios, map scales, mixing chemicals, and financial analysis all use ratios to express proportional relationships.
