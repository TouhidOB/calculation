What Does Converting a Fraction to a Decimal Mean?

It means expressing the same value using place-value notation instead of a ratio. The fraction 3/8 and the decimal 0.375 are identical quantities. A fraction is, at heart, a division problem: the bar means "divide." So 3/8 literally means 3 divided by 8, and the conversion calculation performs that division.

How Do You Convert 3/8 to a Decimal?

Divide the numerator 3 by the denominator 8. The result is 0.375, a terminating decimal: 30÷8 = 3 r6, 60÷8 = 7 r4, 40÷8 = 5 r0. The division terminates when the remainder reaches zero. The calculator performs this instantly, including cases where the long division runs many digits.

Why Do Some Fractions Repeat?

Some fractions produce repeating decimals because the division never reaches a zero remainder. A fraction terminates only when its reduced denominator has no prime factors other than 2 and 5 — the primes behind base 10. Otherwise the decimal repeats, like 1/3 = 0.333... The calculator shows the result to high precision either way.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 3/8 as a decimal?

0.375. Divide 3 by 8, which terminates cleanly after three places. It's common in trades, where 0.375 inch and 3/8 inch are used interchangeably.

Why do some fractions give repeating decimals?

When the denominator (in lowest terms) has prime factors other than 2 and 5. For example, 1/3 repeats because 3 isn't a factor of 10.

Are fractions and decimals always equal?

Yes. 3/8 and 0.375 are equal in every sense. The only difference is notation — fractions use a ratio, decimals use place value.
