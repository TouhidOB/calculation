Slope and Y-Intercept Calculator: How Do You Find a Line Equation?

Given two points, you can describe the straight line through them with a single equation, y = mx + b. Finding the slope and y-intercept by hand means careful subtraction and substitution — easy to flip a sign on. The Slope and Y-Intercept calculator computes both from two points and builds the line equation in one calculation.

Key Takeaways
  • The slope-intercept form is y = mx + b.
  • Slope m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) — rise over run.
  • The y-intercept b is where the line crosses the y-axis (x = 0).
  • The calculator derives m, b, and the full equation from two points.

What Is the Slope of a Line?

The slope measures a line's steepness — how much y changes for each unit of x, computed as rise over run: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). A positive slope rises left to right, a negative slope falls, and zero is flat. For points (1, 2) and (3, 8): m = (8−2)/(3−1) = 6/2 = 3. This is the core of every line calculation.

How Do You Find the Y-Intercept?

Once you have the slope, substitute one point into y = mx + b and solve for b. Using (1, 2) and m=3: 2 = 3(1) + b, so b = −1. The y-intercept is where the line crosses the y-axis. The calculator performs this substitution automatically, giving the equation y = 3x − 1.

Where Are Line Equations Used?

Linear equations model constant-rate relationships everywhere: cost versus quantity, distance versus time, temperature conversions. The slope represents the rate of change, and the y-intercept the starting value. Any time two quantities relate proportionally with a fixed offset, this calculation describes them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is slope-intercept form?

y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept. It's the most common way to write a line because both key features are visible at once.

What if the two x-values are equal?

The line is vertical and the slope is undefined, because dividing by (x₂ − x₁) = 0 is impossible. Vertical lines are written as x = constant.

What does a negative slope mean?

The line falls from left to right — as x increases, y decreases. It represents an inverse rate of change between the two quantities.
