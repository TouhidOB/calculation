What Is Cash Runway?

Cash runway is the length of time, in months, that a company can keep operating before it exhausts its cash, assuming spending and revenue stay constant. It directly answers the question every startup must face: how long do we have? Calculating it is the most important survival calculation a young company performs.

How Is Net Burn Calculated?

Net burn subtracts monthly revenue from monthly expenses, isolating the true cash drain. If you spend $80,000 and earn $30,000 monthly, your net burn is $50,000. This is the figure that erodes your reserves each month. The calculator derives net burn first, then uses it for the runway calculation.

How Can You Extend Your Runway?

Two levers extend runway: cut expenses or grow revenue. Reducing net burn from $50,000 to $25,000 doubles your runway from the same reserves. Raising revenue does the same by shrinking the gap. The calculator lets you test scenarios, showing how each change buys more months in one calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when runway reaches zero?

The company runs out of cash and cannot pay its obligations. Founders aim to raise capital or reach profitability well before that point.

What if revenue exceeds expenses?

Then net burn is negative — the company is cash-flow positive and has effectively infinite runway, no longer depleting reserves.

How is this different from a burn rate calculator?

They're closely related. This tool emphasizes runway (months remaining) derived from net burn; both measure how fast cash depletes.
