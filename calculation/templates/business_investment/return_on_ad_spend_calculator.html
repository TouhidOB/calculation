Calculate your **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**—the revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising. This is a crucial metric for measuring campaign profitability.

ROAS Formula:

$$\text{ROAS (Ratio)} = \frac{\text{Revenue Generated from Ads}}{\text{Total Advertising Cost}}$$
