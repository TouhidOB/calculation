💼 Working Capital Calculator

Calculate **Working Capital**—the difference between a company's current assets and its current liabilities. This is a key measure of **short-term liquidity**.

Working Capital Formula:

$$\text{Working Capital} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}$$
