What Is Conversion Rate?

Conversion rate is the share of visitors or interactions that complete a target action, expressed as a percentage. It applies to any funnel step — adding to cart, subscribing, or purchasing. As a core marketing metric, it shows whether your messaging, design, and offer actually persuade people to act, making the calculation essential to growth.

How Is Conversion Rate Calculated?

Divide the number of conversions by the total number of visitors, then multiply by 100. If 50 of 2,000 visitors make a purchase, the conversion rate is (50 ÷ 2,000) × 100 = 2.5%. The calculator performs this calculation instantly, giving a clear measure of funnel effectiveness.

Why Do Small Improvements Matter?

Conversion gains compound across all your traffic. Lifting a rate from 2% to 3% increases results by 50% without spending a cent more on traffic. That's why marketers obsess over A/B testing and optimization. Tracking the calculation reveals which changes truly move the needle on revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good conversion rate?

It varies widely by industry and goal. E-commerce often sees 2-3%, while landing pages can reach 10% or more. Compare against your own baseline.

What counts as a conversion?

Any action you define as success: a sale, email signup, form submission, download, or click. The calculator works for any goal.

How can I improve my conversion rate?

Clarify your value proposition, simplify the path to action, build trust, and test variations of headlines, buttons, and layouts.
