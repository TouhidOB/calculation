🔗 Click-Through Rate (CTR) Calculator

Calculate your **Click-Through Rate**—the percentage of people who click on your ad or link after seeing it, measuring your content's immediate appeal.

CTR Formula:

$$\text{CTR (\%)} = \frac{\text{Total Clicks}}{\text{Total Impressions}} \times 100$$

Input Performance Data

views

The total number of times your ad, link, or email was displayed to users.

clicks

The number of times the ad, link, or email was clicked by users.
